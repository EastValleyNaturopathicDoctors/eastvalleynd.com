<strong>Laura Schwartz, NMD</strong>

Naturopathy

<hr />

Dr. Laura Schwartz NMD, is a licensed naturopathic doctor in the state of Arizona. Dr. Schwartz graduated with highest honors in 2014, from the Southwest College of Naturopathic Medicine (SCNM) in Tempe, AZ. After graduation, she was awarded a residency position at SCNM, and completed 2-years of residency in general family practice. In addition to practicing at East Valley Naturopathic Doctors, Dr. Schwartz continues teaching at SCNM as adjunct faculty.

Dr. Schwartz grew up in Michigan, where she attended Oakland University and graduated summa cum laude with her Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry. She also played NCAA Division 1 women’s golf for Oakland University.
