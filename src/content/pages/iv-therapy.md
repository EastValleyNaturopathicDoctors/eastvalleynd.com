<h2><b>IV and OZONE THERAPY</b></h2>
EVND is proud to present a new Nutrient IV Therapy Menu to our clinic. We continue to offer a variety of IVs to our current patients and are now making them available to non-established patients who may be interested in benefiting from this service as well. We have a variety of Nutrient IV options based on the particular individual’s needs. IVs are a perfect way to deliver specific nutrients to the body. It is also ideal for those who have digestive issues and may not absorb supplements or nutrients from food properly.
