<h2>Adrenal Hormones</h2>

<p>Your adrenal glands are responsible for producing hormones that help your body respond to stress, regulate energy production, support immune function, maintain healthy blood pressure, and influence metabolism.</p>

<p>When adrenal hormones become imbalanced, many patients experience ongoing fatigue and a reduced ability to recover from physical or emotional stress.</p>

<p>The adrenal glands produce two major stress hormones, adrenaline and cortisol, that help us respond to daily challenges of life without becoming overwhelmed. They also help us engage in the protective fight, flight, or freeze mechanisms when danger presents.</p>

<p>Chronic physical, emotional, or environmental stress may place prolonged demands on the adrenal glands and the central nervous system.</p>

<p>Chronic or lingering stress strains the adrenal gland and central nervous system (CNS), leading to a slow depletion of cortisol and the stress catecholamines, epinephrine (adrenaline), and norepinephrine (noradrenaline). Low levels result in fatigue and a feeling of being too easily overwhelmed by what used to be normal or average stressors. Other symptoms that may coexist with poor stress response are poor sleep quality or a lack of restorative sleep.</p>

<p>At East Valley Naturopathic Doctors (EVND), we evaluate adrenal health alongside thyroid hormones, sex hormones, and other key laboratory markers to better understand how stress may be affecting your overall health.</p>

<p>Lozenges are a combination of oral and sublingual absorption which allows hormones to release in small doses into the bloodstream. Common hormonal combinations include estradiol, estriol, testosterone and/or progesterone.</p>

<h3>Topical Applications (Creams and Patches) ADD</h3>

<p>Topical therapy is a popular option for many patients because it is easy to apply and avoids injections.</p>

<p>Creams and patches are the most favorable route of administration of BHRT, due to lack of discomfort of an injection, and ease of application. These are the most effective as they avoid the pass through the liver and are absorbed directly into the bloodstream. In addition, they seem to mimic the natural rhythm of hormone secretion that occurs in the human body throughout the day. Common hormonal combinations of creams can include estradiol, estriol, testosterone and/or progesterone. Patches are used to deliver estrogen only. (ADD)</p>
