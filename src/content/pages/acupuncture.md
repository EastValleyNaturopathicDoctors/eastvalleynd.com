<h2>What Is Acupuncture?</h2>

<p>The intent of acupuncture therapy is to promote health and alleviate pain. This form of medicine has been used successfully in China for over 2,500 years and is now finally gaining popularity in the U.S. Acupuncture is based on the concept of energetic balance. Energy is thought to run smoothly through channels or meridians in a healthy individual. When there is an energetic imbalance, illness often ensues.</p>

<p>Traditional Chinese Medicine describes this energy as <strong>Qi (pronounced "chee")</strong>, which flows throughout the body along pathways known as meridians. When this flow becomes blocked or unbalanced, pain and illness may develop. Acupuncture works by stimulating specific points along these meridians to encourage the body's natural healing processes and restore balance.</p>

<p>Our acupuncture treatment at East Valley Naturopathic Doctors in Mesa, AZ is based on assessing the flow and distribution of this vital energy within its pathways, and influencing health and sickness by stimulating certain areas along these meridians. Traditionally, these areas, or "acupoints," are stimulated by fine, slender needles.</p>

<p>Modern research has also demonstrated that acupuncture can:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Stimulate the release of endorphins, the body's natural pain-relieving chemicals</li>
  <li>Reduce inflammation</li>
  <li>Improve circulation</li>
  <li>Relax tight muscles</li>
  <li>Support nervous system regulation</li>
  <li>Encourage the body's natural healing response</li>
</ul>

<p>This combination of ancient healing principles and modern scientific understanding makes acupuncture an excellent option for many patients seeking non-surgical, drug-free pain relief.</p>

<h2>What to Expect During Your Acupuncture Appointment</h2>

<p>Many patients are surprised by how relaxing acupuncture feels.</p>

<p>Acupuncture needles are solid and extremely thin (about the width of a human hair). They are sterile and used only once and then discarded, so there is no risk of infection from this treatment.</p>

<p>When the acupuncture needle contacts the energy channel at specific points on the body, the sensation is felt as a mild to moderate dull aching sensation or a tingling sensation. This initial discomfort usually passes very quickly. After the needles are inserted, the patient simply relaxes for twenty to thirty minutes.</p>

<p>Most patients find treatments calming and many even fall asleep during their session.</p>
