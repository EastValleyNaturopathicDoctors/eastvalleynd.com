<h2>Patient Portal – FollowMyHealth</h2>

<h3><em>Notice: Portal Update</em></h3>
<p>Our practice is transitioning from <strong>Patient Fusion</strong> to the new <strong>FollowMyHealth</strong> portal. This update will provide a more modern, secure, and user-friendly way to access your health information and communicate with your provider.</p>

<h2>Log In to Your Patient Portal</h2>
<p>Use your <strong>FollowMyHealth</strong> account to securely access your health information anytime.</p>
<p><strong>Log In to <a href="https://www.followmyhealth.com/Login/Home/Index?authproviders=0&amp;returnArea=PatientAccess#!/default">FollowMyHealth</a></strong><br />
(Access available 24/7 on computer, tablet, or smartphone)</p>
<p>If you have trouble logging in or did not receive your invitation, please contact our office:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Phone:</strong> (480) 906-2534</li>
<li><strong>Email:</strong> <a>patientcare@eastvalleynd.com</a></li>
</ul>
<h2>Quick Access: What You Can Do</h2>
<p>Once logged in, you can:</p>
<ul>
<li>View portions of your medical record and lab results</li>
<li>Securely message your provider about your treatment plan or prescriptions</li>
<li>View upcoming appointments</li>
<li>Complete required forms</li>
</ul>
<h2>How to Get Portal Access</h2>
<p>Within <strong>24 hours of your scheduled visit</strong>, you will receive an email invitation to create or connect your <strong>FollowMyHealth</strong> account.</p>
<ol start="1">
<li>Open the email invitation and follow the link to create your account.</li>
<li>Complete the on-screen steps to verify your information.</li>
<li>Once connected, you can log in anytime from our website’s <strong>Patient Portal</strong> tab.</li>
</ol>
<p>If you do not receive an email invitation, please contact our office and we will resend it.</p>
<h2>Important Notes About Portal Messaging</h2>
<p>The patient portal is intended for <strong>treatment-related questions only</strong>.</p>
<p>For scheduling appointments, lab draws, shipping questions, supplement availability, or other non-treatment concerns, please contact our front office:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Phone:</strong> (480) 906-2534</li>
<li><strong>Email:</strong> <a>patientcare@eastvalleynd.com</a></li>
</ul>
<h2>Previously Used Patient Fusion?</h2>
<p>Our practice has transitioned from <strong>Patient Fusion</strong> to <strong>FollowMyHealth</strong>.</p>
<ul>
<li>Patient Fusion accounts are no longer active for our practice</li>
<li>Previous usernames and secure messages do not transfer</li>
<li>You must use your <strong>FollowMyHealth email invitation</strong> to access your records</li>
</ul>
<p>If you are unsure whether you already have access or need a new invitation sent, please contact us.</p>
