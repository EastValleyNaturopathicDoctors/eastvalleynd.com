<h2>Adrenal Function Testing</h2>

<p>Adrenal function testing assesses how well the adrenal glands manage stress by measuring key hormones like cortisol and DHEA-S. Chronic stress can lead to adrenal exhaustion, progressing through stages where cortisol levels first rise, then eventually drop, causing hormone imbalances and fatigue. This testing helps identify the body's adrenal health and guides treatment to restore balance and support overall wellness. </p>

<h2>ASD Insight™ by NeuroQure</h2>

<p>Traditional autism evaluations are largely based on behavior, communication, and developmental milestones, which means clear answers may not come until well after the earliest months and years of life. NeuroQure’s ASD Insight™ test offers a different path by looking for a biological marker associated with autism risk, giving families the opportunity to learn more far earlier than standard behavioral assessment alone.</p>

<h2>Candida, Yeast, and Fungal Overgrowth (SIFO)</h2>

<p>Candida is a yeast that naturally lives in the body, particularly in the GI tract. Overgrowth, often triggered by antibiotics, sugar-heavy diets, or toxins, can cause symptoms like fatigue, brain fog, and digestive issues. Testing for Candida overgrowth is done through blood tests to assess the body's immune response. Treatment typically involves dietary changes, such as cutting out sugar, and using antifungal medications or supplements to restore balance and reduce symptoms. </p>

This testing complements traditional food allergy assessments by revealing reactions that can trigger histamine responses. <h2>Folate Receptor Antibody Test (FRAT®)</h2>

<p>The Folate Receptor Antibody Test (FRAT®) detects antibodies that block folic acid transport to the brain, essential for development. These antibodies are linked to cerebral folate deficiency (CFD) and Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), hindering folate delivery across the blood-brain barrier. Leucovorin, a folate form that bypasses this blockage, has improved ASD symptoms. The FRAT® also identifies antibodies associated with sub-fertility and neural tube defects in pregnancy, emphasizing the need for early detection and intervention. </p>

<h2>IgE Food and Inhalant Allergy Panels</h2>

<p>IgE Food and Inhalant Allergy Panels detect allergic reactions to common foods and environmental allergens like grass, dust, and molds. Symptoms range from stomach pain and itchy skin to asthma and fatigue. The tests use blood serum panels covering 46 environmental allergens and 96 food allergens, including nuts, dairy, shellfish, and more. These panels are suitable for all ages, including children, requiring just one vial of blood. Desensitization therapy, such as customized allergy drops, can help reduce allergic responses effectively. </p>

<h2>IgG Food Intolerance</h2>

<p>IgG Food Intolerance Testing identifies delayed food reactions that are hard to detect without testing. These Type II reactions can lead to symptoms like migraines, fatigue, and digestive issues. A simple blood test pinpoints food sensitivities, enabling patients to remove problematic foods from their diet. Eliminating these foods can alleviate chronic symptoms, boost energy, and improve mental clarity. The test offers personalized results that help optimize health by reducing the impact of foods regularly consumed. </p>

<h2>Micronutrient Testing</h2>

<p>Micronutrient Testing measures 40 key nutrients at both extracellular and intracellular levels to identify true deficiencies and guide supplementation. It supports individuals with chronic fatigue, weakened immunity, digestive disorders, and more, offering targeted interventions to improve health and well-being. This test is especially useful for those with poor diets, athletes, and people dealing with conditions like hair loss or fibromyalgia. </p>

<h2>Mold and Mycotoxin Testing</h2>

<p>Mold and Mycotoxin Testing identifies mold exposure through the MycoTOX Profile, which tests for 11 mycotoxins from 40 mold species in a urine sample. Mold can be present in water-damaged buildings or certain foods, potentially triggering Chronic Inflammatory Response Syndrome (CIRS) in genetically susceptible individuals. The test is useful even for those no longer exposed to mold, as it can persist in the body. Completing the Mold Exposure Questionnaire helps determine if testing is necessary.</p>

<h2>Methylation Pathways and SNP’s (MTHFR)</h2>

High doses of folic acid or 5-MTHF, along with vitamins B2, B6, and B12, can support the folate cycle. A simple blood test can confirm the presence of this polymorphism, enabling personalized treatment plans for improved health and mental well-being. </p>

<h2>Estrogen Metabolism Index</h2>

<p>The Estrogen Metabolism Test measures the ratio of two estrogen metabolites, 2-hydroxyestrone and 16 alpha-hydroxyestrone, in a urine sample. This “2:16” ratio is linked to the risk of estrogen-sensitive cancers, such as breast and cervical cancer, with higher ratios indicating lower risk. Elevated 16 alpha-hydroxyestrone levels can suggest increased cancer or autoimmune disease risk. The Dutch test is used to evaluate this ratio and other hormone levels, offering valuable insight into disease risk and potential dietary improvements. </p>
