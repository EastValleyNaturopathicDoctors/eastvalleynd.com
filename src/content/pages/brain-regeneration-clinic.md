<p>The brain is the most complex organ in the body. It controls how we think, feel, and function, and while the skull offers real protection, the brain itself is still vulnerable to physical, chemical, and metabolic damage. Optimal brain function doesn't happen automatically. Mood disorders often don't respond to standard therapy or medication; focus and attention can be trained, and conditions like dementia and Alzheimer's disease are increasingly understood to be preventable and, in some cases, reversible. Chronic stress, illness, head trauma, emotional trauma, food additives, herbicides, and environmental toxin exposure all take a toll on the brain over time.</p>

<p>The Brain Regeneration Clinic brings together a range of brain healing therapies, including ExoMind TMS, Neurofeedback, Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy, Frequency Specific Microcurrent, Photobiomodulation, and transcranial Direct Current Stimulation, backed by decades of research supporting improved brain function, cognition, mood, and social engagement.</p>

<p>Every recovery program starts with a baseline QEEG brain map, which evaluates hyper- and hypo-functioning across Delta, Theta, Alpha, and Beta brainwaves. These patterns give us a functional picture of cognitive performance, mood regulation, and even localized brain inflammation, which lets us recommend the therapies most likely to help. Brain mapping is typically repeated after treatment to measure progress and confirm each therapy is working as intended.</p>
