<p>Coronary Artery Disease is a leading cause of morbidity and mortality worldwide, necessitating a multifaceted approach for accurate assessment and management. The integrative approach involves combining various diagnostic modalities to obtain a holistic understanding of CAD. Rather than relying on a single method, this approach leverages the strengths of multiple techniques to enhance accuracy and tailor treatment strategies. This is followed by addressing root issues with Naturopathic therapies to reduce risk and restore optimal functioning.</p>

<p>The following are key components of the integrative approach:</p>

<h2>Clinical Assessment</h2>

<ul>
<li aria-level="1">Thorough evaluation of medical history, risk factors, and symptoms provides crucial insights. Identifying patient-specific factors guides further diagnostic decisions.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Non-Invasive Imaging</h2>

<ul>
<li aria-level="1">Technologies such as coronary CT angiography and stress echocardiography help visualize the coronary arteries, assess blood flow, and identify potential blockages or plaques.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Functional Testing</h2>

<ul>
<li aria-level="1">Stress testing, including exercise or pharmacological stress, uncovers abnormalities in blood flow during increased demand, aiding in the detection of CAD.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Biomarkers</h2>

<ul>
<li aria-level="1">Blood tests measuring cardiac biomarkers like troponin and creatine kinase-MB assist in diagnosing myocardial injury and evaluating the extent of CAD.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Invasive Coronary Angiography</h2>

<ul>
<li aria-level="1">When necessary, coronary angiography provides direct visualization of coronary arteries, allowing for precise identification and localization of blockages.</li>
</ul>

<p>The integrative approach for evaluating Coronary Artery Disease represents a paradigm shift in precision medicine improving diagnostic accuracy, providing tailored treatment plans, and enhancing risk stratification. By combining various diagnostic tools, clinicians can offer personalized, effective, and targeted interventions, ultimately improving patient outcomes in the management of CAD.</p>
