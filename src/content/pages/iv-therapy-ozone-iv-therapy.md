<h2>UVBI Therapy</h2>
<p><strong>Ultraviolet Blood Irradiation (UVBI)</strong> is a gentle, non-pharmaceutical IV therapy that uses the power of ultraviolet light to support immune health, reduce inflammation, and improve circulation.</p>
<p>UVBI involves drawing a small portion of your blood, passing it through a special chamber where it's briefly exposed to ultraviolet (UV) light, and then returning it to your bloodstream. This process mimics the body’s natural immune response — safely activating immune cells and enhancing oxygen delivery to tissues.</p>

<p>Patients often describe UVBI as “a reset for the immune system.” It’s especially supportive during times of chronic infection, lingering post-viral symptoms, or when the immune system feels depleted. <p>Treatment is comfortable and typically lasts 30–60 minutes. For enhanced outcomes, UVBI is often combined with ozone (O3) therapy, major autohemotherapy (MAH), or other IV infusions depending on individual needs.</p>
