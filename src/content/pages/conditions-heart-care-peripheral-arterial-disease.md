Peripheral Arterial Disease (PAD) is a condition that affects the blood vessels outside the heart and brain, primarily those that supply the limbs. Our integrative approach combines traditional medical assessments with holistic strategies to provide a thorough understanding of the patient's condition. The first step involves state-of-the-art diagnostic techniques such as Doppler ultrasound and ankle-brachial index measurements to assess blood flow and detect arterial blockages. These conventional methods are complemented by non-invasive imaging modalities like magnetic resonance angiography and computed tomography angiography for a detailed anatomical evaluation.  Beyond traditional diagnostics, our integrative approach embraces a Naturopathic perspective. Lifestyle factors such as diet, exercise, and stress management play a crucial role in PAD. Our team of experts collaborates with cardiology specialists, interventional radiologists, nutritionists, physical therapists, health coaches, and stress management specialists to design personalized interventions.

<strong>Nutritional Optimization</strong>

Tailored nutrition forms the cornerstone of our approach. We emphasize a plant-based, anti-inflammatory diet rich in antioxidants, omega-3 fatty acids, and essential nutrients. These elements contribute to reducing inflammation, supporting arterial health, and fostering overall well-being.

<strong>Detoxification</strong>

With the environmental impact of microplastics and toxic metals effecting circulation and plaque development, it becomes imperative to engage in thoughtful and targeted testing and treatments to assess and then reduce toxic body burdens. Once individualized testing is complete, a natural and stimulating combination of detox therapies and supplements can improve overall circulation and reduce cardiovascular risk.

<strong>Lifestyle Modification</strong>

Exercise is medicine. Our naturopathic practitioners design personalized exercise regimens, combining cardiovascular activities with targeted exercises to improve circulation and promote vascular health.

<strong>Stress Management</strong>

Stress management techniques such as heart rate training, breathwork, mindfulness meditation, and yoga are also incorporated to address the mind-body connection.

Harnessing the power of nature, we integrate herbal supplements known for their vasodilatory and anti-inflammatory properties. Ginkgo biloba, garlic, and hawthorne are among the botanical allies that may enhance blood flow and alleviate symptoms.
