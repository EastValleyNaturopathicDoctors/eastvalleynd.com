<h2>Insurance Reimbursement Policy</h2>
Naturopathic Physicians may have some costs reimbursed by some PPO plans as out-of-network physicians. We encourage our patients to be aware of their coverage and benefits, both for in-network and out-of-network benefits.  Please know, that we, East Valley Naturopathic Doctors, are not contracted providers through any insurance plans or insurance companies.  Furthermore, Medicare does not cover Naturopathic Medicine.
