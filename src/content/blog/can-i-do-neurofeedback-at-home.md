<h2>Is EVND too far a drive for regular treatment? Now you can get in-home neurofeedback sessions for yourself and your family!</h2>
For years, we at East Valley Naturopathic Doctors have provided Neurofeedback therapy in our office. We now have the ability to guide our patient's neurofeedback sessions from the comfort of their own home!
<h3>How does it work?</h3>

With ongoing advancements in technology and cloud-based programming, we are now able to offer the convenience of our in-home neurofeedback training. After a brain map (QEEG) is completed and protocols established home training can begin. Entertaining options to view while doing training include educational videos, music, movies, and games to make each session engaging and successful. Each session is remotely monitored and recorded just as they are when doing in-office training to review and ensure proper training feedback. Progress is tracked and communication maintained with your neurofeedback specialist offering support and encouragement every step of the way.

Not everyone is a candidate for in-home training and can only be determined after a QEEG is completed and reviewed with your physician. Most cases require some prior in-office training to ensure proper placement of equipment for neuro training. We often encourage those who can, to come at least once weekly in-office, with the remainder sessions done at home. This new and convenient technology opens the door for everyone to have the advantage of Neurofeedback training.
