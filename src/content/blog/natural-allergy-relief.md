<h2>Natural Allergy Relief - No More Pills</h2>

<p>Spring is a beautiful time of year.  However, this season brings with it the seasonal allergies that afflict many people.  The runny nose, itchy throat and headache that is often associated with allergies is enough to send many people running to the nearest pharmacy.  But relief doesn't come in a package of chemicals; there are natural ways to alleviate the allergy symptoms and the allergy response itself.</p>

<p>First, it is important to evaluate your  personal health habits, especially during allergy season.  Staying in good overall health helps the body to have the strength to fight off irritants.</p>

<p>Natural Allergy Relief for Yourself</p>

<ul>
<li>As always, be sure to drink plenty of water.</li>
<li>Eat healthy foods.</li>
<li>Get adequate sleep.</li>
<li>Exercise regularly.</li>
</ul>

<p>Next, you should evaluate the situation in your home and make any necessary modifications.  Reducing your exposure to allergens in your home will be a great help to you.</p>

<h3>Natural Allergy Relief for your Home</h3>

<ul>
<li>Make sure that the air filters are changed frequently.</li>
<li>Keep pets out of bedrooms to reduce fur and dander in sleeping spaces.</li>
<li>Keep windows closed to block the entrance of outside allergens.</li>
</ul>
