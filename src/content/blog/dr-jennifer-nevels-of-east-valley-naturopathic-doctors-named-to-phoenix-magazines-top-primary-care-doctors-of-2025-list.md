<p>Phoenix, AZ – March 2025 – <a href="/">East Valley Naturopathic Doctors</a> proudly announces that <a href="/physicians/jennifer-nevels/"><strong>Dr. Jennifer Nevels</strong> </a>has been recognized as one of the <a href="https://www.phoenixmag.com/2025/03/08/top-primary-care-doctors-of-2025/"><strong>Top Primary Care Doctors of 2025</strong></a> by <em>Phoenix Magazine</em>, earning distinction in the Top Naturopathic Doctors category. This prestigious list, compiled through a secure peer-based voting process, highlights the most trusted and respected medical professionals in the Valley.</p>

<h2>A Leader in Naturopathic Medicine</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Practicing at one of the largest naturopathic medical centers in East Mesa, Dr. Nevels has built a reputation for providing compassionate, patient-centered care. Her expertise spans a wide range of health concerns, including <a href="/conditions/womens-health/menopause/">menopause</a>, <a href="/conditions/womens-health/breast-implant-illness/">Breast Implant Illness</a>, <a href="/conditions/womens-health/hormonal-fluctuations-and-mood-disorders/">mood disorders</a>, <a href="/conditions/hashimotos-and-thyroid/">hypothyroidism</a>, <a href="/conditions/chronic-fatigue/">chronic fatigue</a>, autoimmune disease, poor digestion, skin conditions, and <a href="/conditions/chronic-pain/migraines-and-tension-headaches/">migraines</a>. Her personalized approach to medicine allows her to treat the root causes of illness while empowering patients to take charge of their health.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dr. Jennifer Nevels is a licensed naturopathic medical doctor in the state of Arizona and a highly regarded expert in <a href="/conditions/womens-health/">women’s health</a>. As a general family practitioner, Dr. Nevels has dedicated her career to helping patients achieve optimal health through naturopathic medicine, which emphasizes natural remedies to support the body’s ability to heal and prevent illness.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>Extensive Education and Expertise</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dr. Nevels’ impressive career began with a Bachelor of Science in Psychology from the University of Arizona, followed by a Doctorate of Naturopathic Medicine from Southwest College of Naturopathic Medicine (SCNM) in 2003. Her commitment to excellence led her to complete a one-year residency specializing in women’s medicine, further honing her expertise in treating hormone imbalances and other women’s health concerns.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Following her residency, Dr. Nevels remained deeply involved in medical education as a faculty member at SCNM, where she served as the Interim Chair of the Women’s Department. In this role, she taught gynecology courses, supervised medical students, and contributed to research on Bio-Identical Hormone Replacement Therapy and Herbal Formulations for Menopausal Symptoms. Today, she devotes her full attention to patient care at East Valley Naturopathic Doctors, where she continues to transform lives through naturopathic healing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
