<p>Marie Ybarra is a licensed aesthetician with over 15 years of experience in the skincare and beauty industry. She offers personalized, results-driven treatments that focus on restoring skin health and confidence. Her areas of expertise include advanced facials, chemical peels, microdermabrasion, waxing, makeup application, and microneedling using the FDA-cleared SkinPen device.</p>

<p>Based at East Valley Naturopathic Doctors, Marie takes a holistic yet clinical approach to skincare. She combines thorough skin analysis with client education, helping each individual understand their skin and how to care for it beyond the treatment room.</p>

<p>Marie holds certifications in GM Collin, PCA Skin, Skin Script RX, and Manual Lymphatic Drainage, and received her microneedling certification in 2024. As an Arizona native, she also specializes in treating skin affected by dry, desert conditions.</p>

<p>Marie is passionate about creating a comfortable, clean, and welcoming environment where clients can relax, recharge, and walk away with healthy, glowing skin.</p>
