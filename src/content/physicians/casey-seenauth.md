<b>Dr. Casey Seenauth</b> is a naturopathic physician licensed in Arizona. He graduated from Sonoran University of Health Sciences (formerly Southwest College of Naturopathic Medicine, SCNM) in 2013 and completed two years of general medicine residency. He previously served as Staff Physician at SCNM's Neil Riordan Center for Regenerative Medicine, where he supervised medical students and resident physicians while maintaining a private practice. He is also among a handful of naturopaths to complete advanced post-graduate training in regenerative injection therapies.

Dr. Seenauth's practice centers on general and metabolic health — helping patients manage cardiovascular disease and cholesterol, blood sugar and metabolic syndrome, weight, energy and fatigue, and digestive issues, using an individualized approach that draws on nutrition, botanical medicine, homeopathy, mind-body medicine, and lifestyle intervention for men and women alike. He also has a particular area of focus in men's health, including hormone optimization, libido, and erectile dysfunction. Alongside this, he treats acute and chronic pain and musculoskeletal injury using regenerative injection therapy (prolotherapy and platelet-rich plasma) and acupuncture — skills he's applied to his own injuries as a former NCAA tennis player and lifelong active individual.

Dr. Seenauth takes the naturopathic principle of&nbsp;<i>docere</i>&nbsp;(doctor as teacher) to heart. Education is a cornerstone of his approach, and amid a flood of conflicting health advice and internet trends, he helps patients separate signal from noise — drawing on clinical experience and sound judgment to guide decisions, rather than chasing whatever's trending. He takes time to ensure his patients are well-informed participants in their own health. He has been a guest speaker at the Phoenix VA, Barrow Neurological Institute, and the US Congress, educating other healthcare providers about naturopathic medicine.

He lives in Mesa with his wife and their cat, Jesse.
