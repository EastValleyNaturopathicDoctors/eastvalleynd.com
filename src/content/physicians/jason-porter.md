<p>Dr. Jason Porter is a 2003 graduate of the Southwest College of Naturopathic Medicine (recently renamed Sonoran University) in Tempe, Arizona, USA. He is the co-founder of East Valley Naturopathic Doctors and the Brain Regeneration Clinic both located in Mesa, Arizona. Dr. Porter is credited for development of a precision based clinic for brain rehabilitation and for optimization of peak performance using Biofeedback techniques including EEG targeted Neurofeedback therapy as a Board Certified Neurotherapist (BCN). </p>

<p>Before completing naturopathic medical school, Dr. Porter attended Arizona State University where he completed his bachelors in Exercise Science. Dr. Porter has advanced training courses in Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT), Biofeedback and Neurofeedback Therapy  (Board Certified), Genetics, physical manipulation, ozone therapy, nutrient IV therapy, Stem cell, Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP) and Prolotherapy, IV chelation therapy(nationally certified), environmental medicine, hormone replacement, Lyme disease, sports medicine, and nutrient/amino acid therapies for optimal brain functioning. Dr. Porter’s education and professional training continues through many organizations including the International Society on Neurofeedback and Regulation (ISNR).</p>

<p>In his private practice, Dr. Porter focuses on the treatment and recovery from chronic illness by restoring function to the endocrine, immune, and central nervous systems. He has developed a unique process for evaluating and treating chronic illness, that allows severely ill patients to recover from hopeless and confusing conditions.  Dr. Porter has been able to infuse naturopathic medicine modalities with Neurofeedback and Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy to improve cognition, mood, energy, sleep, and stress response, allowing chronically ill children and adults to return to optimal brain functioning.</p>

<p>Dr. Porter resides in Gilbert, Arizona, USA, with his wonderful wife, five children, two awesome daughter-in-laws, and three very cute grand-daughters.</p>
